## Rewind notes

- Rewind: every other card of its suit on your side goes back to your hand; the Rewind stays, and so do other Rewinds (otherwise two Rewinds, or a Mimic copying one, could bounce each other forever). 10-card hand limit applies.
- Rewind players win **46.9%**, the lowest effect, just above a plain card (46.5%). It trades points on the table for cards in hand, and playing it when you're behind keeps you behind.
- The computer's handling barely matters: Hard mirror with the returned cards valued at 0, 3 (shipped) or 6 per card gives 46.5%, 47.9%, 47.8%.
- 0 stuck games in 36,000; average game length 43.3 actions.
- Ideas if Renato wants it stronger: the Rewind also returns itself, or you draw 1 if nothing comes back, or it only takes back the top 2 cards (keeping more points on the table).
